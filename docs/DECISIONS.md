# Decision log

Append-only. Any change to truth definitions, thresholds, scoring or binning after a batch exists needs a dated entry here and a new pre-registration.

## 2026-10-22

- **D-001 Manipulated variable.** Literature density per gene, holding task and dataset fixed. Reason: post-cutoff designs saturate (BioStudyBench best closed model 94.7%) and planted faults saturated in planted-fault-bench.
- **D-002 Domain.** Genes over neuroimaging: measurable density for ~10,000 uniformly perturbed genes; neuro entities lack a clean density axis.
- **D-003 Primary dataset.** Zhu et al. 2026 CD4+ T-cell genome-scale CRISPRi Perturb-seq: donor replication (truth from held-out donors) and three conditions (context shift). Virtual Cell Challenge 2026 data as contingent replication.
- **D-004 Density measure.** Fractional gene2pubmed counts, papers linking >100 genes dropped, Entrez IDs, PMID cutoff at the earliest evaluated model's training cutoff.
- **D-005 Phase 0 truth.** Combined-donor differential-expression results with fixed thresholds as provisional truth; held-out-donor truth starts in Phase 1.
- **D-006 Integrity.** Batches require a filled, committed and pushed pre-registration; one batch per pre-registration commit unless a reason is recorded in the ledger; only aggregates are committed; data, runs and truth live outside the repository.
- **D-007 Zero-paper bin.** With `density.zero_bin: true`, QC-passing genes with no gene2pubmed-linked papers before the PMID cutoff (after the >100-gene filter) form bin 0; the remaining genes get `density.n_bins` quantile bins of log density, numbered 1..n_bins, with ties sharing a bin. Reason: zero-paper genes all tie at the lowest density, so plain quantiles merge them into a bin of variable size and blur the least-studied end. Decided before any batch and before the gene table was built.
- **D-008 Stray records in the PMID cutoff.** The cutoff is the smallest PMID entered on the cutoff day after ignoring PMIDs more than `density.stray_pmid_gap` (1,000,000) below that day's median; the number ignored is recorded in `density_meta.json`. Reason: on 2026-07-01 one old record (PMID 25905264) carries that entry date while the other 6,424 start at 42378835, and taking the plain minimum would drop about eleven years of papers. Decided before any batch.
- **D-009 No keep_test_genes requirement.** `keep_test_genes` appears only in the pseudobulk object; the DE results file contains only DE-eligible perturbations (11,526 targets vs the paper's 11,527 tested genes), so the flag is redundant and `obs.flags_require_true` is empty. `ontarget_significant` (on-target knockdown significant at 10% FDR, per perturbation × condition) is reported per density bin in the feasibility report as a covariate and is not used for filtering. Decided before any batch.
- **D-010 Zero-paper bin dropped (supersedes D-007).** `density.zero_bin` is false: bins are 5 quintiles of log density over all QC-passing genes, with zero-paper genes in the lowest quintile. Reason: bin 0 (123 zero-paper genes) had only 3 "decreased" and 13 "increased" candidate questions on the readout panel, too few for label-balanced sampling. Decided from candidate counts only, before any model call. IL17A is removed from `probe.readout_panel` because it is not measured in the DE file.

- **D-015 Probe v2 scope: perturbations with donor-pair fits.** Donor-pair DE fits (`GWCD4i.DE_stats.by_donors.h5mu`) exist only for 4,880 perturbation × condition rows (2,591 targets), all with at least 30 downstream DE genes. Probe v2 uses them for truth agreement, so only covered perturbations are eligible as targets, as F-neighbour correct options and distractors, and as both members of F-gene pairs; the nine readouts without donor-pair fits are dropped. Density bins stay defined over all QC-passing genes and covered genes are sampled within them. Scope: probe v2 measures gene knowledge among perturbations with clear transcriptional effects (~1,921 QC-passing genes; 17–26% coverage by bin, rising with density); covered genes differ from typical genes in expression (median target baseMean 49 vs 28) and knockdown reliability (significant on-target knockdown 93% vs 78%). For documentation: the 178 QC-passing rows with 30+ DE genes but no donor-pair fit have far fewer cells (median 40 vs 364), more single-guide estimates (28% vs 9%) and fewer significant on-target knockdowns (52% vs 93%); per-donor cell counts would need the pseudobulk object and were not checked.
- **D-016 Probe v2 design rules fixed before generation.** (1) Strata: F-readout condition × label × readout responsiveness class (per condition, terciles of the number of other QC-passing perturbations that change the readout, padj < alpha and |LFC| ≥ lfc_min, combined-donor DE); F-gene condition × label; F-neighbour condition. Fallback: if any F-readout condition × label × class cell cannot fill in every bin, drop the class and stratify on condition × label only. (2) Donor-agreement filter, computed three ways: (i) agreement in all three disjoint splits; (ii) D1+D2 vs D3+D4; (iii) D1+D4 vs D2+D3 (batch-balanced). After the per-cell counts are set in the pre-registration, use the first of (i), (ii), (iii) that fills every stratum cell.
- **D-017 Per-model density cutoffs (probe v2).** Each model gets its own literature cutoff: opus-5.5-cli 2026-07-01 (June 2026 knowledge cutoff; PMID cutoff 42378835, 1 stray record ignored), sol-6.1-cli 2026-05-01 (April 30, 2026; PMID cutoff 42060948, 5 stray records ignored). `scripts/02_build_density.py --cutoff-date D` writes `density_<D>.parquet`; the gene table carries a log-density and bin column per cutoff. Strata are sampled from the earliest cutoff's bins; each model is analysed against its own cutoff's log density. A secondary T-cell density counts only gene2pubmed papers indexed with the "T-Lymphocytes" MeSH heading (exploded); PubMed returns at most ~10,000 IDs per query, so the single search per cutoff is run as date-bisected sub-queries (393,372 and 395,161 PMIDs before the two cutoffs).
- **D-018 Probe v2 design.** Prior-only questions asked twice, with the gene named and with it hidden ("a gene whose identity is hidden"; both genes hidden in F-gene), run in a shuffled order so a question's calls are never adjacent, k samples per prompt. Families: F-readout (transcriptome-wide readouts; unchanged readouts respond to at least R other perturbations), F-gene (matched pairs: which knockdown changes more genes, same bin and condition, matched on target baseMean, ratio and donor-half order agreement required), F-neighbour (which of four knockdowns is most similar; tiers easy, hard and discovery; discovery excluded from the pre-registered checks). Phase 0 genes (522) are excluded as development data; flagged genes form a separate positive-control stratum. Primary estimand: Brier(hidden) − Brier(named) per question, aggregated per bin and pooled with gene-clustered bootstraps; samples combined either as the mean per-sample Brier or the Brier of the mean forecast (both computed; one pre-registered). Baseline ladder: uniform/label frequencies, hidden version, fame-only (log density + target expression, cross-fitted on outside-batch genes with strata-balanced training), annotation-only (GOA human annotations dated before each model's cutoff, excluding those citing PMID 42664972). GAF dates are last-modified dates, so the date filter also drops electronic (IEA) annotations re-dated by later releases; the dry run reports how many. Program-level questions are deferred to Phase 1 (programs must come from donors 1–2 only). sol-6.1-cli runs on a pre-registered fraction of the questions, sampled with the same strata, as a contamination contrast (its cutoff precedes the 28 May 2026 data release). The scorer writes a final report only when every model has 0 unanswered calls and 0 infrastructure errors.
- **D-019 F-readout strata use target effect size (amends D-016).** The readout-responsiveness class is replaced by terciles of target effect size: the number of downstream DE genes in the combined fit (`n_downstream`), with terciles computed over covered targets within each condition. Strata: condition × label × effect tercile, with the same fallback (drop the tercile if any cell cannot fill in every bin). Changed after the count and effect-size diagnostics of the dry run (the donor-agreement filter keeps about 1–5% of changed readout questions and shifts them toward strong perturbations, more in dense bins) and before any model call.
- **D-020 F-neighbour similarity on informative readouts.** Similarity, its donor-half versions and self-reliability are computed over a fixed informative readout set per condition: the `n_inf` readouts most often changed among covered perturbations (padj < alpha and |LFC| ≥ lfc_min, combined fit). Over all ~10,000 readouts, self-reliability had a median near 0.1, so the earlier fixed grids admitted almost no items. Self-reliability is the minimum over the splits the agreement option uses. The dry-run grids are data quantiles: self-reliability at the 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles of covered perturbations; the margin at the 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles of candidate items' gaps. The correct option must still rank first among the four in every split used. Options and distractors never include flagged genes; whether they also exclude Phase 0 genes is a FILL (`probe2.neighbour.options_exclude_phase0`), with capacity reported both ways. Decided from dry-run diagnostics, before any model call.
- **D-021 GO from archived releases (supersedes the date filter in D-018).** Each model uses the archived GOA human release published closest before its cutoff, with no date-column filter: release 234 (generated 2026-04-30) for 2026-05-01 and release 236 (generated 2026-06-17) for 2026-07-01; SHA-256 in `data_root/raw/go/MANIFEST.tsv` and in each batch manifest. Annotations citing the dataset paper are still dropped (none in these releases). The main annotation-only baseline includes IEA annotations; a no-IEA variant is reported as a secondary result. Reason: GAF dates are last-modified dates, so the date filter removed every IEA annotation and about 254,000 others re-dated by later releases.
- **D-022 Flagged stratum pooled; harness workers.** The flagged (positive-control) stratum is pooled across density bins with no per-bin balance: strata condition × label (F-readout, F-gene) and condition (F-neighbour), analysed pooled and excluded from the pre-registered A and B checks. F-gene flagged pairs are one flagged gene plus an unflagged partner from the same density bin, expression-matched, and not used anywhere in the main stratum; F-neighbour flagged items have a flagged target and unflagged options. Per-cell counts are a FILL (`probe2.flagged_per_cell`). opus-5.5-cli runs with 2 parallel calls (4 caused sign-in failures in Phase 0). CLI calls get an empty stdin (`codex exec` otherwise waits on an open stdin).
- **D-023 F-readout filter preference: effect-size strata first (amends D-016 and D-019).** F-readout uses effect-size-stratified sampling under the strictest donor-agreement filter that fills it, ahead of any filter without terciles. Order: (i) all three splits with terciles, (ii) D1+D2 vs D3+D4 with terciles, (iii) D1+D4 vs D2+D3 with terciles, then (i), (ii), (iii) without terciles; the first that fills every cell at the pre-registered per-cell count is used. At the dry-run capacities this puts (ii) with terciles (up to 5 per cell) ahead of (i) without terciles (up to 8). F-gene and F-neighbour keep the D-016 order. Also from this round: the annotation-only and fame-only rungs are calibrated probabilistic forecasts fitted on genes outside the scored set (multinomial logistic for F-readout and F-gene; for F-neighbour a softmax over GO Jaccard with its temperature fitted, replacing the one-hot pick); F-neighbour main-stratum results are descriptive, outside analyses A and B; F-gene results are also reported by DE-count ratio band (2–4, 4–10, >10), descriptively. Made from dry-run diagnostics, before any model call.
- **D-024 Probe v2 gain in skill points.** Per question, gain = (Brier(hidden) − Brier(named)) / uniform Brier for the family (F-readout 2/3, F-gene 1/2, F-neighbour 3/4). Gate A's pooled gain, the top-bin check and the positive control weight F-readout and F-gene equally (mean of the two family means); B regresses per-question normalized gains with family as a covariate; per-family results are descriptive. Reason: raw Brier differences are not comparable across families with different numbers of options. Two optional additions are implemented behind config switches, off unless pre-registered: a wrong-name control (`probe2.wrong_name`; a third prompt naming a different real gene from the same bin, expression-matched; knowledge gain = Brier(wrong name) − Brier(named), in skill points; +50% calls) and top-effect readouts (`probe2.readout.top_k_effects`; changed readouts only from the target's top K effects by |z|). Also: the donor-agreement filter is set per family (`probe2.agreement.chosen` maps family to filter); F-neighbour's self-reliability and margin thresholds are given as quantiles (`min_self_corr_quantile`, `margin_quantile`) and applied per condition for the chosen split and n_inf, with the margin quantile taken over the condition's main-stratum candidate items and the absolute values recorded in the batch manifest; F-neighbour per-cell counts are set per tier; flagged F-neighbour items are limited to `flagged_tiers`. Decided before any model call.
